<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>What’s up VOYAGE fam</strong>,<br><br></p><p style="text-align: center;">We hope you’re doing well.</p><p style="text-align: center;"><br></p><p>Last week we went out and fucked around a little and decided to take on a 22 km hike that was supposed to take about six to seven hours. A couple of days earlier, we had talked to a friend and told him we were aiming for a sub-5, maybe even a sub-4. Let’s just say he was a classic hater. <br></p><p>The drive was about an hour, the weather was perfect, and everything felt easy… until it didn’t. When we checked the route on Komoot and saw the elevation profile, we realized there was an almost straight climb to the top. Around 1300 meters of elevation gain over roughly 10 kilometers. Of course, we kind of ignored that on the way there. We got humbled very quickly. The start was, let’s say, a bit rough. But we found our groove after about 30 minutes and really started pushing. It was still a fun day, not a serious attempt, but the more we went on, the more we laughed about our friend doubting us. <strong>22 km</strong> and <strong>1300 meters</strong> of elevation gain later, we were back at the car in <strong>3 hours and 15 minutes</strong>. </p><p>Now let’s see what the next adventure has in store for us. <br><br>In the meantime, <em>go find your next adventure</em>. <br><br>It might be gone if you wait too long…</p>
